Privacy & Security

Understand where your MoneyLeak information is stored.

Your financial information is stored locally.
MoneyLeak uses your browser's local storage for the information you enter into the application. Your transactions, goals, budgets and preferences are not automatically uploaded to a MoneyLeak server.

Backup recommendation: Export your data regularly if the information is important to you. Keep your backup file somewhere secure.

Permanently remove financial information stored in this browser.

Delete all financial data
